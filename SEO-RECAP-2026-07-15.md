# What We Did Today, and Why It Matters

_SEO ranking push · everydayfuture.work · 2026-07-15 · working checklist: `SEO-ACTIONS.md`_

## The problem we started with

Four weeks after launch, the site wasn't showing up for the two searches that matter: **"Taylor Winters Seattle"** and **"conscious executive coaching."** A full live audit found that nothing on the site was broken — titles, schema, sitemap, robots, redirects all verified working. The real problem was **identity fragmentation**: Google couldn't confidently connect "Taylor Winters" to "Every Day Future" because the strongest signals about her pointed elsewhere — her LinkedIn advertised the old brand ("LTW Executive Coaching"), and her old domain (ltw.coach) was still alive in Google's index, *competing with the new site* because its redirect was configured as temporary (302) instead of permanent (301).

The audit also surfaced genuine good news: Google's own data (Search Console) showed the site already ranking **~page 2 (position 18.9) for "conscious executive coaching," with 283 impressions** — meaning Google understands what the site is about and the term is within reach. The gap was rank, not visibility.

## What we fixed (all verified live)

1. **ltw.coach now permanently redirects (301, https) to everydayfuture.work.** Before: a 302 that told Google "keep the old site indexed." After: a permanent move — Google will drop ltw.coach from results and transfer its accumulated authority to the new domain over the next few weeks. *This was the single biggest fix.*
2. **Taylor's LinkedIn fully rebranded.** Headline → "Founder & Conscious Executive Coach · Every Day Future"; current position → Every Day Future (company page linked); About rewritten to lead with the practice (original safely backed up); website field → everydayfuture.work; new on-brand banner replacing the old "Creative Entrepreneurs" one. Her LinkedIn is the #1 result for her name — it now sends people (and Google) to the right place.
3. **A real backlink from benjamindwinters.com** — "my wife Taylor Winters' conscious executive coaching practice," with her name as the link. One sentence that ties the entity together from an independent, established site.
4. **Indexing requested in Search Console** for the site's key pages (5 of 7 done; 2 essay URLs remain for the next sitting — daily quota).
5. **"Taylor Winters" added to the homepage meta description** — deployed to production today. Her name previously appeared in no title or description on the site; now the homepage's search-result snippet says "Conscious executive coaching with Taylor Winters…"
6. **Bonus discovery:** her old LinkedIn About revealed credentials we weren't using — **ICF Master Certified Coach (MCC**, held by ~4% of ICF coaches**)** and **UPenn Positive Psychology**. Queued to add to the site's structured data (B6).

## Why this works

Google ranks *entities*, not just pages. Every fix today made the same statement from a different, independent place: **Taylor Winters = Every Day Future = conscious executive coaching = Seattle.** Old domain → 301 → new domain. LinkedIn → new brand → new site. Husband's site → her name → her site. Homepage snippet → her name. When the signals agree, Google consolidates them into one confident answer instead of splitting attention across two brands and two domains.

## What to expect

- **"Taylor Winters Seattle": ~2–6 weeks.** Name queries are low-competition; this was a plumbing problem, now fixed. ltw.coach results fade as Google recrawls.
- **"Conscious executive coaching": ~2–4 months to page 1.** Already position ~19; what moves it now is content and reputation — not more fixes.
