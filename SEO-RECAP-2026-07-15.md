## 1. Positioning — how "conscious executive coaching" was chosen (June 4–11)

The single biggest SEO decision was made before any code: **what phrase should this practice own?**

- Bare head terms ("executive coaching" ≈ and "leadership coaching" ≈ 18k searches/mo) are unrankable for a new solo practice — they're supporting terms, not targets.
- "Conscious leadership coaching" was ruled out: it's owned by the Conscious Leadership Group (the "15 Commitments" book/ecosystem) — an established player we'd never displace.
- **"Conscious executive coaching" — Option B, locked June 4** — is the calculated adjacency: it borrows the conscious-leadership interest while sitting on the executive-coaching demand, and it describes what Taylor actually does.
- **A deep-research pass (June 11; 18 sources, 25 claims adversarially fact-checked) validated it:** only one boutique competitor (Inductive, Boulder CO) targets the exact phrase; no USPTO trademark exists (it's a descriptive phrase — owning it is an SEO race, not a legal risk); no Seattle competitor uses it; and CLG doesn't target "executive coaching" at all. **Contested but winnable.**
- The research also set the playbook: buyer-intent queries ("executive coach near me," "Seattle executive coach") live in Google Business Profile, not site pages; long-tail problem-framed content beats head terms for a solo practice; nobody searches modality terms (IPNB/NLP/DBT stay as credibility copy, never keyword targets).

## 2. On-site foundation (June 4, shipped in one pass)

Before this work the site had a bare `<head>` — one title tag, nothing else. Shipped:

- **Per-page titles + meta descriptions** (unique on every page; homepage title: "Conscious Executive Coaching — Every Day Future")
- **Canonical URLs** on every page, built from the production domain
- **Open Graph + Twitter cards** with a branded 1200×630 share image (links now render rich previews in iMessage/LinkedIn/Slack)
- **Favicon set** (EDF wordmark icon — tab, bookmark, home-screen)
- **robots.txt** — allow all crawlers **including AI engines** (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended): being cited by AI answers is a discovery channel we want
- **XML sitemap** auto-generated on every build
- **Structured data (JSON-LD):** FAQPage (the site's 6 Q&As, eligible for rich results), plus a **Person + ProfessionalService graph** — Taylor Winters ("Founder & Conscious Executive Coach"), LTW Coaching LLC, Seattle base with areaServed US + Europe, Berkeley Haas credential, LinkedIn/Instagram `sameAs`
- **The phrase in visible copy:** bio clause "— a conscious executive coaching practice —" (June 4) and the S3 heading "Outcomes from conscious executive coaching" (June 11) — Google weighs headings and body text above meta tags

## 5. The ranking push (July 15 — today)

**The audit:** four weeks post-launch, the site wasn't visible for "Taylor Winters Seattle" or ranking page 1 for "conscious executive coaching." A full live audit verified **nothing on-site was broken** — the problem was **identity fragmentation**: Taylor's strongest signals pointed at her old brand. Her LinkedIn (the #1 result for her name) said "LTW Executive Coaching"; her old domain ltw.coach was still indexed and *competing with the new site* because its redirect was a temporary 302, not a permanent 301.

**The good news in the data:** Search Console showed the site already at **~position 19 (page 2) for "conscious executive coaching" with 283 impressions** — Google understands the site; the gap is rank, not visibility.

**Fixed today (all verified live):**
1. **ltw.coach → 301 permanent redirect to https://everydayfuture.work** — Google will drop the old domain and transfer its authority (~2–4 weeks)
2. **LinkedIn fully rebranded** — headline, current position → Every Day Future, About rewritten (original backed up), website field, new on-brand banner
3. **Backlink from benjamindwinters.com** — "my wife Taylor Winters' conscious executive coaching practice," name-anchored
4. **Search Console indexing requested** for 5 of the 7 unindexed pages
5. **"Taylor Winters" added to the homepage meta description** — deployed to production and live
6. **Credentials discovered** in her old LinkedIn copy — ICF **Master Certified Coach (MCC)** + UPenn Positive Psychology — queued for the site's schema

**Why it works:** Google ranks *entities*. Every fix makes the same statement from an independent place — old domain → new domain; LinkedIn → new brand; husband's site → her name → her site; homepage snippet → her name. When the signals agree, Google consolidates "Taylor Winters = Every Day Future = conscious executive coaching = Seattle" into one confident answer.
